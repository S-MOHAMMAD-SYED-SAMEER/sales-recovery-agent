AI Customer Support & Sales Recovery

Helps online stores answer customer questions instantly, recover hesitant buyers, and reduce repetitive support work.

Ask a question the way one of your customers would. The assistant answers from the store's own written policies, looks up live order, product, and promotion details before it replies, and picks up on a buyer who sounds unsure.

Everything here is safe to try

  • Product availability — is the Ceramic Mug or Wool Scarf in stock?
  • Order status — where is order 1001, 1002, or 1003?
  • Discount validity — is code WELCOME10 still valid?
  • Shipping questions — how long does international delivery take?
  • Return policy — what happens if a customer wants to send something back?
  • A hesitant buyer — "I like it, but I'm still deciding."

Every reply shows what the assistant checked before answering, and what it noticed about the customer. Switch to Operator to see each step the server took.
