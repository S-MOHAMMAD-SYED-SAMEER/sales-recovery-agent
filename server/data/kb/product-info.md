# Product Information & Care

## Ceramic Mug (MUG-001)

Hand-glazed stoneware mug, 12oz capacity. Dishwasher and microwave safe. Available in matte white,
sage green, and terracotta. Because each mug is hand-glazed, slight variations in color and glaze
texture between pieces are normal and not considered defects.

## Linen Tote Bag (TOTE-002)

100% pre-washed European linen, reinforced stitched handles, holds up to 15kg. Machine washable on
a cold, gentle cycle — air dry only, do not tumble dry, as high heat can weaken the linen fibers
over time. Available in natural, charcoal, and dusty rose.

## Wool Scarf (SCARF-003)

100% merino wool, approximately 180cm x 30cm. Hand wash cold or dry clean only; do not machine
wash, as merino wool can shrink and felt in a washing machine. Store folded rather than hung to
keep its shape.

## Travel Candle (CANDLE-004)

Soy wax blend candle in a reusable tin, approximately 25-hour burn time. For an even burn and to
avoid tunneling, let the wax pool across the full diameter of the tin the first time you light it.
Trim the wick to 5mm before each use. Not intended for use in checked luggage due to airline
restrictions on wax candles.

## General Sizing & Materials

All product dimensions listed on the site are approximate and measured by hand, so small
variations (a few millimeters) between individual items are normal. Material composition for every
product is listed on its individual product page.
