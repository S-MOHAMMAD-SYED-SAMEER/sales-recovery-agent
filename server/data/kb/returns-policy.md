# Returns & Refunds Policy

## Return Window

Items can be returned within 30 days of the delivery date for a full refund, provided they are
unused, unwashed, and in their original packaging with tags attached. We are unable to accept
returns after the 30-day window has closed.

## How to Start a Return

Email support with your order number and the item(s) you'd like to return. We'll send a prepaid
return shipping label for domestic orders. International customers are responsible for return
shipping costs unless the item arrived damaged or defective.

## Refund Processing

Once we receive and inspect a returned item, refunds are issued to the original payment method
within 5-7 business days. It can take an additional 3-5 business days for the refund to appear on
a bank or card statement, depending on the customer's bank.

## Exchanges

We don't process direct exchanges. To get a different size or color, return the original item for
a refund and place a new order for the item you want — this is faster than waiting for an exchange
to be processed.

## Non-Returnable Items

Final-sale and clearance items are not eligible for return or refund. This is always noted on the
product page at the time of purchase.

## Damaged or Defective Items

If an item arrives damaged or defective, contact support within 7 days of delivery with a photo of
the issue. We will send a replacement or a full refund, including return shipping, at no cost to
the customer.
