# Shipping Policy

## Domestic Shipping

We ship to all addresses within the country via standard courier. Orders placed before 2pm on a
business day are processed the same day; orders placed after 2pm are processed the next business
day. Standard domestic shipping takes 3-5 business days and costs a flat $4.99, or is free on
orders over $50.

## International Shipping

We currently ship to most countries worldwide, including the United States, Canada, the United
Kingdom, the European Union, Australia, and New Zealand. International orders are processed within
1-2 business days of purchase. Customers are responsible for any customs duties or import taxes
charged by their country — these are not included in the order total and are collected by the
courier on delivery.

## Delivery Times

- Domestic: 3-5 business days after processing.
- International (US, Canada, UK, EU): 7-12 business days after processing.
- International (Australia, New Zealand, other regions): 10-18 business days after processing.

Delivery times are estimates, not guarantees, and can be affected by customs processing in the
destination country. We do not offer expedited or overnight shipping at this time.

## Order Tracking

Once an order ships, a tracking number is emailed to the address used at checkout. Tracking
updates can take 24-48 hours to first appear after a label is created.
