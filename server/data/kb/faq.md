# Frequently Asked Questions

## What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit and debit cards, as well as PayPal. All payments are processed
securely at checkout; we never store full card numbers on our servers.

## Can I change or cancel my order after placing it?

If an order hasn't shipped yet, email support as soon as possible and we'll try to change or
cancel it. Once an order has entered processing (usually within a few hours of being placed), we
can no longer guarantee changes can be made before it ships.

## Do you offer gift wrapping?

Yes, gift wrapping is available at checkout for a flat $3.50 fee, and includes a printed gift note
with a custom message you provide.

## How do I contact customer support?

Email support is the fastest way to reach us, and we aim to respond within one business day. We
do not currently offer phone support.

## Do you have a physical store?

No, we are an online-only store and do not currently operate any physical retail locations.

## Are gift cards available?

Yes, digital gift cards are available in fixed denominations and are delivered by email. Gift
cards do not expire and can be combined with a discount code on the same order.

## Is my personal information kept private?

Yes. Customer information is used only to process orders and respond to support requests, and is
never sold to third parties.
